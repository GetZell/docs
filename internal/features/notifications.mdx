## Overview

Notifications keep users informed about coaching, streaks, training, and platform events. Backend notification templates and delivery jobs support localized messages and deep links.

## Streak Notification Delivery

The `streakSaved` notification is created when a freeze saves a user's streak. Its delivery window applies only to external channels:

| Channel | Delivery timing |
| --- | --- |
| In-app | Created immediately when the notification job runs. |
| Email or Slack | Sent from 08:00 inclusive until 18:00 exclusive; outside that window, queued for the next 08:00. |

The window uses the tenant's `notificationPreferences.dailyReviewReminder.timezone`, defaulting to `Europe/Berlin` when no timezone is configured. The delivery handler checks the current local time whenever a job runs, including delayed jobs. If a delayed job executes outside the window, it defers the external delivery again. Requeued jobs contain only external channels, so they do not recreate the in-app notification.

Other notification types keep their existing schedules.

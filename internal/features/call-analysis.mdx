## Overview

Call Analysis is the detail view for a single call. It combines recording playback, transcript context, evaluation results, feedback comments, inline coaching, and navigation from snippets or history.

## Real-Call Header Metadata

For real calls, the header displays recipient identity, company, date, duration, language, call type, outcome, and owner assignment when those values exist.

Role behavior:

- `USER`: sees language, call type, and outcome as read-only metadata.
- `TEAM_LEAD`: can update language, call type, and outcome from inline header controls.
- `ADMIN` and `INTERNAL_TOOL_ADMIN`: can update language, call type, outcome, title, and owner assignment.

Language, call type, outcome, and owner changes are optimistic in the frontend and only show toast notifications on errors. Successful saves are silent. Title changes still show success and error toasts.

## Navigation Behavior

Call Analysis preserves breadcrumb context when opened from call collections or hiring flows. If a call cannot be loaded after deletion or missing data, the route redirects admins to team reports and regular users to home.

Source reference: `src/components/analysis/useCallAnalysisNavigation.ts`.

## Recording Playback

When a call includes video, Call Analysis displays the recording in a floating player alongside the shared waveform controls. Users can drag the player by its top grip and resize it from the lower-left resize handle. The player starts at 320 pixels wide and is constrained between 240 and 640 pixels, or the available viewport width when that is smaller. With the resize handle focused, the left and right arrow keys change the width in 20-pixel steps.

Hovering over or focusing the video reveals controls to rewind 10 seconds, play or pause, and move forward 10 seconds. Clicking the video also toggles playback. Rewind and forward actions stop at the beginning or end of the recording and preserve the current playing or paused state.

The waveform controls remain available for video and audio recordings. They show the current time and duration and provide playback speed, volume, waveform seeking, and play or pause controls.

## Feedback Anchoring

On the Conversation tab, each feedback card is tied to the transcript turn it refers to. The link comes from the comment's `transcriptIndex` and `transcriptEndIndex`; no extra backend data is needed.

- **Card header.** Each anchored card shows a clickable timestamp (`▶ 01:17`) and the speaker of the first turn in its passage. The timestamp comes from that turn's `secondsFromStart`. The speaker uses the call's speaker names, then the `Speaker N` / `Colleague N` labels, then "Rep" or "Prospect".
- **Passage highlight.** Hovering or selecting a card highlights every transcript turn from `transcriptIndex` to `transcriptEndIndex`.
- **Two-way selection.** Clicking a card selects it and scrolls the transcript to its passage. Clicking the timestamp also seeks the recording to one second before the passage. Clicking a transcript line selects the card that starts on that line, otherwise the narrowest card whose passage covers it; a line with no feedback clears the selection.
- **Layout.** In the side column, unselected cards show three lines of feedback and a one-line reply box (the dictation hint appears only once the box is focused). The selected card expands to show the full text and votes, and is placed level with its passage while the other cards move above and below it. If there is no room above, cards fall back to stacking downwards.
- **Whole-call feedback.** A comment with no `transcriptIndex` is treated as feedback on the whole call. It is shown as a grey "Whole call" card pinned above the other cards, does not tint a transcript line, and adds no marker to the waveform.

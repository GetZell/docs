## Overview

Connect your Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook calendar so the Zell meeting bot can join and record your meetings for analysis.

An organization administrator must enable Meeting Recorder before you can connect a calendar or send a bot to a meeting. If you see **Meeting Recorder is unavailable**, ask your administrator to enable it.

## Connect a Calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Meeting Recorder">
    Open [Meeting Recorder](https://app.getzell.com/settings/user/meeting-recorder) in User Settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Your Calendar">
    Under **Calendar Connection**, click **Connect Google Calendar** or **Connect Microsoft Outlook**.

    The selected calendar shows a connecting indicator. Both choices are temporarily disabled while Zell starts the connection.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorize the Connection">
    Follow the provider's authorization flow for the account you want to connect.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the Connection">
    After returning to Zell, check for **Connected via Google Calendar** or **Connected via Microsoft Outlook**.

    If the connection fails, Zell shows an error. Return to Meeting Recorder and try connecting again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Automatic Recording

Once connected, use **Automatically record external calendar meetings** to control automatic recording. This sends the bot to meetings with external attendees and a supported conferencing link. Internal-only meetings are skipped.

## Record a Meeting Now

To send the bot immediately, paste a meeting URL under **Record a Meeting Now** and click **Record**. This option is available when Meeting Recorder is enabled, even without a connected calendar.

## Disconnect or Change Calendars

Open Meeting Recorder and click **Disconnect Calendar**. After the calendar disconnects, the Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook connection choices appear again. Choose a provider to connect another calendar.

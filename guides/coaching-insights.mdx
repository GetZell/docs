## Open Team Coaching Insights

Open **Reports** and choose **Coaching Insights** to review coaching opportunities across your team. Use the report filters to narrow the people, calls, and time period you want to review.

This guide covers the manager's Reports view. For your own coaching moments and skill gaps, see the [Calls workspace](/guides/calls).

## Find Who Needs the Most Help

The **Who needs the most help** card lists team members from highest to lowest coaching need. Each row shows the person's avatar, name, call count, current streak beside a flame icon, and coaching need score.

An alert icon marks members whose engagement score is below `3.0`. This helps you spot people who may need a reminder to review their coaching feedback.

**Coaching need / 10** measures how much opportunity there is to help. A higher score means more coaching support is needed. Yellow bars show coaching need; a blue row marks the selected person.

The first person is selected when the list loads. Select another person to review their categories in the adjacent card.

## Review Their Lowest-Scoring Categories

The **Their lowest-scoring categories** card shows the selected person's categories from lowest to highest performance.

The card's header repeats their current streak and shows their engagement score from `0.0` to `10.0`. Engagement measures how consistently they review coaching moments. When a previous-period comparison is available and the change is nonzero, an arrow shows whether engagement increased or decreased, alongside the change in score points. Learn how to interpret the score in [Engagement Score](/guides/engagement-score).

**Performance / 10** measures how well the person performed in each category. A higher score means stronger performance, so the categories at the top are the first places to look for improvement.

| Score | How to read it |
| --- | --- |
| Coaching need / 10 | Higher means more coaching support is needed. |
| Performance / 10 | Higher means stronger performance. |

Each category also shows how many coachable moments have been reviewed. If no coachable moments are available for a category, the card says so.

## Review Moments and Set a Focus

Choose **View snippets** on a category with available moments to review the relevant call excerpts and coaching feedback.

Use **Add focus** to mark a category for coaching. You can set more than one focus category. Focused categories show a **Focus** badge; use its **Remove focus** control to clear that focus.

The report's average engagement score remains useful for checking whether the team is reviewing coaching feedback. Learn more in [Engagement Score](/guides/engagement-score).

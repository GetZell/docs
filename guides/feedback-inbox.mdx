## Review a Moment

The daily Feedback Inbox on **Home** helps you review coaching moments from your own calls, one card at a time. Review the feedback and complete the card's interaction to see the result.

## Reply Before Continuing

Use **Reply** to discuss the moment. Sending a reply keeps the current card and its coaching feedback visible so you can follow the conversation with Flare.

After you complete the card, **Continue** becomes available when there is no unfinished reply activity:

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You have typed a reply | Send it or clear the text to continue. |
| Your reply is being sent | Wait for sending to finish. |
| Flare is preparing a response | Wait until Flare responds or the waiting state ends. |
| No draft or reply is pending | Choose **Continue** to load the next moment. |

You can write a follow-up while waiting, but you cannot send another reply until the current reply finishes. A follow-up draft must also be sent or cleared before continuing.

Returning to the browser tab or reconnecting to the network keeps the current card visible. Choose **Continue** when you are ready to move on.

For focused review by skill area, open the [Calls workspace](/guides/calls).

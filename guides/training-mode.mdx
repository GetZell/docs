## Overview

Every day, Zell turns feedback on your own calls into a short daily review of about 5 minutes. Your training mode decides how that review looks.

| Mode | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Minigames** | Quick games on moments from your calls, such as choosing the better reply or spotting the missed moment. |
| **No minigames** | Just your feedback to read, with no games or answers to give. |
| **Mix** (default) | Alternates by day: games one day, plain feedback the next. |

## Changing Your Training Mode

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    At the bottom of the left sidebar, click **Settings** (the gear icon), then **User Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Training Mode">
    In the settings menu, under **Training**, select **Training Mode**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a Mode">
    Click **Minigames**, **No minigames**, or **Mix**. Your choice is saved straight away, with no save button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The change applies to today's review right away. Feedback you already finished today still counts toward your progress.
</Note>

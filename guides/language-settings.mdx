## Overview

Zell supports multiple languages for both the user interface and AI bot interactions. You can configure language settings to match your team's needs and regional requirements.

## Changing Your Language Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    At the bottom of the left sidebar, click **Settings** (the gear icon).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to Language Settings">
    Select **Language**. The **Language Settings** dialog opens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Your Language">
    Choose your preferred language:
    - English
    - German (Deutsch)
    - Italian (Italiano)
    - French (Français)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Close the Dialog">
    The language changes as soon as you select it. Click **Close** to close the dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

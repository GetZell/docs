## Your Daily Review Reminder

The 5 min Daily Review is a short daily habit: open your review, look at feedback from your calls, and choose something to work on in your next conversation.

Zell sends a personal reminder when you have unread feedback ready to review. It includes feedback from one of your actual calls, with a short transcript excerpt when available.

If you have a coaching focus, Zell prioritizes feedback that matches it. If no matching feedback is ready, the reminder uses your latest eligible unread feedback.

Select **Start 5 min Daily Review** to sign in and open your Daily Review on the Zell home page.

## Where You Receive It

When your organization has [connected Slack](/guides/slack-integration), Zell sends the reminder as a direct message. Your Zell and Slack accounts should use the same email address.

Email is the fallback when Slack is unavailable. You do not need to connect Slack to receive reminders.

## Schedule and Settings

For organizations using real call analysis, reminders are enabled by default at **09:00 Europe/Berlin**. Existing custom schedules and disabled reminders are kept.

Admins can manage the reminder in **Tenant Settings → Notification Preferences**:

1. Find **Daily Review Reminder**.
2. Use the switch to enable or disable reminders for your organization.
3. Set **Reminder time**. The timezone used for the schedule appears below the time field.

Changes save automatically. The time follows the displayed timezone, including daylight saving changes.

## Why You Might Not Receive a Reminder

A daily schedule does not mean you receive a message every day. Zell sends at most one reminder per day, and only when there is eligible unread feedback with coaching text from the last 30 days.

You may not receive a reminder if you have already reviewed your feedback, the remaining feedback is too old or marked unhelpful, or your admin has disabled reminders. These reminders are for reps; admin and team lead accounts do not receive them.
